Add the VIPS Hub like an app 📱

On iPhone or iPad, you can add this site to your Home Screen so it opens like an app.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the More button, then Share — or tap the Share button directly, depending on your Safari layout.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Turn on Open as Web App.
  5. Tap Add.
If you don’t see “Add to Home Screen,” scroll to the bottom of the Share sheet, tap Edit Actions, and add it there.

Add the VIPS Hub like an app

On an Android device, you can add this site to your Home Screen for quick access.

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu next to the address bar.
  3. Tap Add to home screen.
  4. Choose Create shortcut, then tap Add.
Depending on your Android device and Chrome version, you may see Install instead. If so, tap Install and follow the prompts.
