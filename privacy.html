Privacy Policy

McGown VIPS Hub – Admin Dashboard

In short: The McGown VIPS Hub Admin Dashboard uses Google authentication to identify authorized administrators and requests only the Google access needed to maintain Hub content. It does not sell user data or use Google user data for advertising.

About this application

The McGown VIPS Hub is a volunteer-developed resource intended to help organize and share information useful to the Sue McGown Elementary volunteer community. The Admin Dashboard allows specifically authorized administrators to maintain content displayed by the Hub.

Information we access

The Admin Dashboard may request access to:

  • Your Google Account email address, to identify you and determine whether your account is authorized to use the Admin Dashboard.
  • The Google spreadsheet associated with this application, to read and update Hub content and administrative records.
  • Specific Google Drive files used with this application, such as files created or uploaded through Hub administration features.

The application is configured to use limited Google OAuth permissions. It does not request general access to all Google Drive files or all Google spreadsheets in an administrator's account.

How information is used

Google Account information and authorized Google data are used only to authenticate administrators and operate, maintain, secure, and audit the McGown VIPS Hub and its administrative features.

User data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Access and authorization

Google sign-in alone does not grant administrative access. Administrative access is limited to accounts separately authorized for the Hub. Authorized administrators may have different permissions based on their assigned role.

Storage, retention, and security

Hub content and administrative records may be stored in Google services used to operate the application. Administrative activity may be recorded for accountability and troubleshooting. Information is retained as needed to operate and maintain the Hub and may be removed when it is no longer needed.

Reasonable measures are used to limit administrative access and reduce the permissions requested from Google to those necessary for the application's functions.

Revoking Google access

An administrator may revoke the application's access to their Google Account through their Google Account security settings. Revoking authorization may prevent that administrator from using some or all Admin Dashboard features.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the Hub's features, data practices, or Google permissions change. The current version will be published at this page.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or the McGown VIPS Hub may be directed to the Hub administrator using the support contact displayed during the Google authorization process.

Important notice

The McGown VIPS Hub is developed for volunteers by volunteers. It is not an official Sue McGown Elementary or Cypress-Fairbanks Independent School District (CFISD) website or application.
